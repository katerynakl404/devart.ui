`CardHeader` takes `actions` — a trailing slot one title line tall that centres a link or a button on the title's line and never shrinks; a link there is 14px (`text-sm`). `actionsAlign="center"` centres it on the title and the description together instead, for a card whose one act it is, and moves it under the text, at the start, once the text would have less than 20rem (§162).
