`IconButton` takes a `tooltip` — the action named on hover and focus, the aria-label or a shorter word (§151). New `IconHint` — a status or note glyph beside a name with its own tooltip, in Alert's tones (§152). New `ClampedText` — a text cut to N lines with See more only when it is cut, in place or handed over (§153). `CardFooter` takes `divided` and keeps the card's bottom corners while `sticky` (§123).
